> Chasm City Cable Car

  • A pod with four telescopic arms climbing through vines hanging from a futuristic city, inspired by Alastair Reynolds' Chasm City. Written in C++ and SDL3, compiled to WebAssembly.

  • The vines are simulated with position based dynamics, the pod's weight pulls them down.

  • A controller distributes the force needed to carry and steer the pod onto the grasping arms, while the gait lets go of weak grips and reaches for better ones.

  • The pod follows the mouse cursor.
  • Click into the window to get access to additional functionalities.
  • Press 'c' to toggle the controller.
  • Press 'f' to toggle path finding.
  • Press 'w' to toggle the wind.
  • Press 'r' to respawn the pod, 'g' for a new city.

> Robot Arm

  • A two-link robot arm written in C++ and SDL2.

  • Langragian equations for a double pendulum define the dynamics of the system.

  • Inverse kinematics are calculated to retrieve the joint angle necessary for the end-effector to reach the desired target defined by the mouse cursor.

  • A non-linear controller defines the behaviour of the two motors at the joints, which can alilily a limited torque to the pendulum.

  • Click into the window to get access to additional functionalities.
  • Press 'c' to toggle the controller.
  • Press 't' to toggle the trace.
